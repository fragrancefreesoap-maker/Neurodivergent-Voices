Accounts & Secure Sync

This is the account-ready entry point for the Academy. Student and teacher records should only be synchronized after a secure identity/database provider is connected.

Privacy by design

  • No password is collected by this page.
  • Local classroom data can continue to be used without an online account.
  • Online student records require a properly configured authentication and database service with access rules before they are enabled.
