Free Wellness Tool

My Neurodivergent Wellness Check-In

A simple daily check-in to pause, notice what you need, and make one thing easier.

  • ☐ How is my energy?
  • ☐ Am I overwhelmed?
  • ☐ Is my environment comfortable?
  • ☐ Have I eaten?
  • ☐ Have I had enough water?
  • ☐ Do I need quiet?
  • ☐ Do I need movement?
  • ☐ Do I need connection?
  • ☐ What can I make easier today?

Our Mission

Different doesn't mean less.

Neurodivergent Voices exists to make information easier to find, support easier to access, and neurodivergent voices harder to ignore.


We believe people should be able to learn about themselves without shame, find tools that work for them, and know that they aren't alone.

Built with accessibility in mind.

Clear language. Simple navigation. Strong contrast. Keyboard-friendly controls. Responsive design and reduced-motion support.
