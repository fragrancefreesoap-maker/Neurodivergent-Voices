ABOUT

Different minds. Real voices.

Neurodivergent Voices is a growing resource, education, tools, and community project designed to make useful information easier to find and everyday support easier to access.

Our mission

We believe neurodivergent people should be able to find information without shame, learn in ways that respect different needs, communicate what works for them, and see their experiences represented with dignity.

What you'll find here

  • Organized resources for autism, ADHD, learning differences, education, employment, advocacy, and families.
  • Free practical tools for planning, wellness check-ins, sensory awareness, and self-advocacy.
  • Neurodivergent Voices Academy with Pre-K through Grade 5 general education learning pathways.
  • Community perspectives that recognize that neurodivergent experiences are diverse.

Accessibility matters

The site is being built with clear language, readable layouts, keyboard-friendly controls, responsive design, strong focus indicators, and reduced-motion support. Accessibility is an ongoing process, so we continue to improve the experience.

Information boundaries

Neurodivergent Voices provides educational and informational resources. It does not provide medical, mental-health, or legal advice. External resources are provided as starting points; users should review information directly with the relevant organization or qualified professional when appropriate.
